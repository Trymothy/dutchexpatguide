Contact

Found an error in one of our guides? Want to suggest a topic we haven't covered, or have feedback on something we got wrong? We'd genuinely like to hear from you.

Write to us at contact@hollandexpatguide.com. We read every message and aim to reply to genuine questions and corrections within a few working days.

Corrections

Accuracy matters most to us on this site. If you spot something that is wrong or out of date — a changed fee, a superseded rule, a broken official link — please tell us and point us to the source if you can. We take corrections seriously and fix genuine errors quickly. You can read more about how we research and review our guides in our editorial standards.

What we can and can't do

We are happy to point you toward the right official resource or clarify how something on the site works. We cannot, however, give personalised legal, tax, immigration or financial advice, or act on your behalf with any Dutch authority. For decisions specific to your situation, please consult the relevant official body or a qualified professional.
