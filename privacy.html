Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Who we are

Expat in Holland is an independent website providing practical guides for internationals in the Netherlands. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you visit this site.

Data we collect

We do not collect personal data directly. We do not have user accounts, contact forms that store submissions, or email newsletters at this time. The only data collection on this site occurs through third-party services described below.

Google AdSense

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

For more information about how Google uses data when you use its partners' sites or apps, visit Google's Privacy & Terms.

Cookies

This site itself does not set cookies. Google AdSense and Google Analytics (if enabled) may set cookies on your device. These are third-party cookies governed by Google's privacy policy. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings or through Google's consent tools.

Your rights under GDPR

If you are located in the European Union (including the Netherlands), you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Since we do not collect personal data directly, these rights apply primarily to data held by Google as a third-party processor.

To exercise your rights regarding Google's data, visit your Google Account or contact Google directly.

For questions about this privacy policy, contact us at: contact page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
